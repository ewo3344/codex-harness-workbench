Re-entrant daemon config baseline verification
===============================================

Changed branch/field
--------------------
`DaemonConfigStore.patch()` and `DaemonConfigStore.reload()` now advance
`lastKnownPersisted` before `applyReplacement()` notifies live owners. A synchronous
`onChange` listener can therefore issue a nested patch without the outer operation
overwriting the nested operation's persisted baseline.
